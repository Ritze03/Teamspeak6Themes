# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories:

**Added** (new features),
**Fixed** (bug and behaviour fixes),
**Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## 2026-11-09

### Added
- **Website**: a redesigned website with a live preview of every theme variant.
- **Amoled Monochrome**: a new pure black color variant.

### Fixed
- **Theme preview**: switching quickly between variants no longer breaks the preview.

## 2026-07-09

### Added
- **First release**: two compact TeamSpeak 6 themes, Graphite and Aurora, with eight
  color variants and the tight, space-saving feel of TeamSpeak 3.
- **Website**: an English and German website with install instructions.
- **Download button**: a direct download of all themes as one ZIP.

### Info
- **Restart**: TeamSpeak needs a restart to apply the smaller channel tree.
