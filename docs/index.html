This is a snapshot of the real TeamSpeak 6 client with made-up names, and the themes load straight from this repository. Every variant also comes as a NoScroll version without scrollbars in the channel tree and chat, e.g. Vanilla - NoScroll.

Installation

Takes about two minutes. No installer, you just copy two folders.

  1. Download the themes and unpack the ZIP.
    Download ZIP
  2. In TeamSpeak, open Settings → Appearance and turn on User Theme.
  3. Click the small folder icon next to the theme cards.

    TeamSpeak opens its extensions folder for you. Usually:

    Linux  ~/.config/TeamSpeak/Default/extensions Windows  %APPDATA%\TeamSpeak\Default\extensions
  4. Copy the folders de.wurst.graphite and de.wurst.aurora into it.

    You'll find them inside the unpacked download. Installing only one of them works too.

  5. In TeamSpeak, click the refresh icon (circular arrow) next to the folder icon.

    The new themes appear as cards. Click the one you like.

  6. Restart TeamSpeak.

    The channel tree only picks up the new row sizes after a restart. Done!

Restart once when you switch to these themes from TeamSpeak's own look or another theme, and again when you switch back. Until then the channel list can look squished or oddly spaced. Switching between Graphite and Aurora variants works right away, since they all share the same sizing.

Tree style

Made for Compact under Settings → Appearance → Tree Style. Detailed works too.

Updating

Replace both folders with the new versions, then press refresh or restart TeamSpeak. If TeamSpeak switched to another theme, just pick yours again.

Uninstalling

Delete the folders from the extensions folder.
